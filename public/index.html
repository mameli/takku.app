Takku takku ← taccuino,
Italian for notebook

Records your meetings, transcribes them on your Mac and writes the notes into your Obsidian vault.

You choose who writes the summary: a model on your Mac, or a provider you trust. Made for people whose company doesn’t let meeting audio and text leave the EU.

brew install --cask mameli/takku/takku

Or download the latest version from GitHub. Free and open source. Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.

What it does

  • Records both sides of a call: your microphone and the call’s audio, from Meet, Zoom, Teams or any other app. ⌃⌥⌘R starts and stops it from any app
  • Transcribes on your Mac with Whisper or Parakeet, in Italian, English and 23 other European languages.
  • Tells the speakers apart by voice, and names them when the call makes it clear, or when you write the names in the note.
  • Writes Markdown into Obsidian: the note opens when the meeting starts, the summary goes on top, the transcript in a file of its own.
  • Marks the moments that matter: press ⌃⌥⌘M and that passage gets a ⭐ in the transcript and priority in the summary.
  • Names the note after the calendar event in progress and lists who was invited, if you allow it.
  • Offers to start when a call app takes the microphone, and to stop when it lets go.
  • Keeps templates and a vocabulary as notes in your vault: write how the summary should look, and the names that speech recognition gets wrong.

A meeting, from start to stop

  1. Start from the menu bar, with ⌃⌥⌘R, or from the notification when a call begins.
  2. Write your own notes in the meeting note that opens in Obsidian. Takku never changes them, and uses them to steer the summary.
  3. Stop. Takku finishes the transcript on your Mac.
  4. The summary appears at the top of the note, each point with the time it comes from, followed by a link to the transcript. A notification tells you when it’s ready.

Where your data is

Audio
On your Mac, deleted after 7 days.
Notes and transcripts
In your vault, as plain Markdown files.
Summary
Written by a model on your Mac (llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio) or by the OpenAI-compatible provider you choose, for example one hosted in the EU. It receives the transcript and your notes, never the audio.
API keys
In the macOS Keychain.
Nothing else
The transcription model is downloaded once. A daily update check asks GitHub for the latest version and sends nothing about you or your meetings.

Recording a meeting may need the consent of the others: tell them.

Install

  1. Run brew install --cask mameli/takku/takku, or download the zip and move Takku to Applications.
  2. Open Takku. macOS says it can’t verify the app, because Takku is signed but not notarized by Apple: press Done. notarizing needs a paid Apple developer account
  3. In System Settings, Privacy & Security, press Open Anyway next to Takku and confirm with your password.
  4. Choose your vault and a summary provider in Settings. The first setup guide walks you through it.
