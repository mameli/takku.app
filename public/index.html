Takku takku ← taccuino,
Italian for notebook

Meeting notes in your Obsidian vault. The audio never leaves your Mac.

Takku records both sides of a call, transcribes it on your Mac and writes the summary at the top of your note. An alternative to Granola for people and companies that care where their meeting data goes.

Download for Mac

Free and open source, for Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later.

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask mameli/takku/takku

  • The audio stays on your Mac

    Whisper or Parakeet transcribe the call right on your Mac. No recording is ever uploaded.

  • You choose who writes the summary

    A model on your Mac, or a provider you trust, for example one in the EU. It reads the transcript, never the audio.

  • Notes where you already write

    The note opens in Obsidian when the call starts. Takku keeps what you write and uses it to shape the summary.

  • Any call, in your language

    Meet, Zoom, Teams or any other app, in Italian, English and 23 more European languages. It tells the speakers apart.

Everything Takku keeps and sends is on where your data goes. Other questions are answered in the FAQ.

A meeting, from start to stop

  1. Press ⌃⌥⌘R, or start from the notification Takku shows when a call begins. tell the others you’re recording
  2. Write your own notes in Obsidian while you talk. Press ⌃⌥⌘M to star a moment that matters.
  3. Stop. A notification tells you when the summary is ready at the top of the note, each point with the time it was said.

Install

  1. Download Takku and move it to Applications, or run brew install --cask mameli/takku/takku.
  2. Open it: a welcome window asks for your vault and the permissions, and gets the transcription model ready.
  3. For summaries, choose a model in Settings. The setup guide has examples.
