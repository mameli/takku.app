Takku

Where your data goes

Takku runs on your Mac. This page lists everything it keeps, everything it sends, and to whom.

Takku has no account, no analytics and no server of its own, except Takku Credit if you choose it. It’s open source, so all of this can be checked in the code.

What stays on your Mac

The audio
Kept in Takku’s folder, about 20 MB per hour of meeting, and deleted after 7 days. You can keep it from 1 to 90 days in Settings, Recordings.
The transcription
Whisper or Parakeet turn speech into text on your Mac, and the voices are told apart on your Mac too.
Notes and transcripts
In your vault, as plain Markdown files. Your templates and vocabulary are Markdown files too, in ~/.takku.
Screenshots
Only the ones you take with ⌃⌥⌘S, in your vault, in Meetings/Screenshots. Takku never deletes them and never sends them anywhere.
The summary log
A copy of every request sent to the summary provider and of every reply, in ~/.takku/summary-log, to help you improve your templates. Takku never reads it or sends it anywhere, not even in a problem report. It keeps up to 5 MB, oldest first out; turn it off or delete it in Settings, Recordings.
API keys
In the macOS Keychain.
Your calendar
Read through macOS, only if you turn it on.

What leaves your Mac

To the summary provider
The transcript, your notes, the template, the terms of your vocabulary and, with the calendar on, the names of who was invited. Never the audio, nor your screenshots. With a model on your Mac, such as llama.cpp, Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves at all. With Takku Credit, see below.
From Hugging Face
The transcription model and a small model that tells voices apart, downloaded once. Nothing about you or your meetings is sent.
To your hook
Only if you set one up: the note and the transcript go wherever your script sends them, such as Notion. Takku doesn’t check where. See Hooks.
To GitHub
Once a day Takku asks for the latest version. The request carries nothing about you. Turn it off in Settings, General, Check for updates.

With Takku Credit

Takku Credit is optional: only if you use it.

Your meetings
The transcript and your notes go to api.takku.app, which passes them to TensorX and the summary back to you. Neither keeps them: TensorX processes them in memory on servers in the EU, with AWS in Ireland and Scaleway in France.
What Takku Credit keeps
Your email address, the balance, and for each summary how many tokens it used and what it cost. Not the key itself, only a fingerprint of it, and never the text of a meeting.
Who else is involved
Cloudflare runs api.takku.app. Stripe takes the payment and sends the receipt. Resend sends the email with your key.

Choosing a provider

Takku doesn’t check where a provider processes your data. If you or your company have rules, use a model on your Mac, or a provider whose terms fit them, for example Regolo.ai in Italy or Mistral in France. Read the terms before using it for real meetings.

The setup has profiles to start from. Takku Credit uses a model hosted in the EU and keeps nothing of your meetings.

Recording other people

Recording a meeting may need the consent of the others. Tell them you’re recording, and follow the rules that apply to you.

This website

No cookies, no scripts and no trackers. The fonts are served from the site itself.
