Takku

Where your data goes

Takku runs on your Mac. This page lists everything it keeps, everything it sends, and to whom.

Takku has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. It’s open source, so all of this can be checked in the code.

What stays on your Mac

The audio
Kept in Takku’s folder, about 20 MB per hour of meeting, and deleted after 7 days. You can keep it from 1 to 90 days in Settings, Recordings.
The transcription
Whisper or Parakeet turn speech into text on your Mac, and the voices are told apart on your Mac too.
Notes and transcripts
In your vault, as plain Markdown files, with your templates and vocabulary.
API keys
In the macOS Keychain.
Your calendar
Read through macOS, only if you turn it on.

What leaves your Mac

To the summary provider
The transcript, your notes, the template, the terms of your vocabulary and, with the calendar on, the names of who was invited. Never the audio. With a model on your Mac, such as llama.cpp, Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves at all.
From Hugging Face
The transcription model and a small model that tells voices apart, downloaded once. Nothing about you or your meetings is sent.
To GitHub
Once a day Takku asks for the latest version. The request carries nothing about you. Turn it off in Settings, General, Check for updates.

Choosing a provider

Takku doesn’t check where a provider processes your data. If you or your company have rules, use a model on your Mac, or a provider whose terms fit them, for example Regolo.ai in Italy or Mistral in France. Read the terms before using it for real meetings.

The setup has profiles to start from.

Recording other people

Recording a meeting may need the consent of the others. Tell them you’re recording, and follow the rules that apply to you.

This website

No cookies, no scripts and no trackers. The fonts are served from the site itself.
