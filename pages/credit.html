How it works

  1. Buy a pack. The key arrives by email within a minute: keep that email.
  2. In Takku, open Settings, Summary Profiles, Takku Credit, and paste the key.
  3. Press Use for Summaries. To top up later, press Add credit there.

Good to know

Price
Packs of 5, 10 or 20 €, VAT included. A summary costs about one cent, and the credit doesn’t expire.
Privacy
A model hosted in the EU writes the summary, and nothing of your meetings is kept. Details.
